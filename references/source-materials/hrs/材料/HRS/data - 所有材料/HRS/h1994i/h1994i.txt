HRS 1994 Imputation Release (Version 3.0)
November 24, 2003

The HRS 1994 Imputation (Version 3.0) is now available. Covering nine sections of 
the HRS 1994 interviews, and containing data for 11,596 respondents and 6,979 
households, this release supersedes the previous release (Version 2.0, May 2001) 
of this data. 

Compared to the previous version, this release includes for the first time the 
imputations for Sections C (Cognition), E (Family Structure), S (Widowhood and Divorce), 
V (Asset Changes), Kid (Kids), and Par (Parents). In addition, the imputation data 
for Sections D (Housing), K (Assets and Debts), and N (Income) that were in the previous 
release have been updated, based on a slightly modified program that is more consistent 
with one used for the imputations in other HRS/AHEAD waves.

Please note that although the most extreme outliers were excluded from the imputation 
process used to fill missing data, they remain in the reported data. Users may wish to 
review distributions and consider the use of robust statistical procedures to minimize 
the impact of any outliers on their analyses. 

The dataset may be downloaded from the HRS Public File Download Area. Registration (free) 
is required for access.

If you find any problems with the data, please report them to us via the Help Desk, or 
by sending e-mail directly to hrsquest@isr.umich.edu.
