1999 HRS Experimental Mailout Data Available

7/24/2000

An experimental data collection was conducted in 1999 with a sample of 2,998
respondents in the 1998 interviews. Questionnaires were returned by 2,454 of
these sample members. Most of the questions replicate those asked in the biennial
interviews. In addition, there are questions about preferences for different possible
spending patterns over time. Data from these questionnaires are now available.

The release consists of 2 files:

1) mailA.txt (this read me file)
2) mailA.zip which contains:
01hr99ma.pdf (Adobe Acrobat questionnaire file)
mailA.da (ASCII data file)
mailA.dct (Stata data definition statements)
mailA.do (Stata data definition statements)
mailA.sas (SAS data definition statements)
mailA.sps (SPSS data definition statements)
mailA.txt (this read me file)
study_description.doc (Word97 study description)
