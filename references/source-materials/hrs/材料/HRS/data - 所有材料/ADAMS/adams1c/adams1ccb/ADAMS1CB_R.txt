CBVISN2        VISUAL ACUITY - 20/(NORMAL EYE SEES AT 20 FT)
         Section: CB    Level: Respondent      Type: Numeric    Width: 3   Decimals: 0
         Ref: VISION

         Category: FOLLOW-UP ASSESSMENT DATA
         Measure: PHYSICAL EXAM
         

         The nurse assessed visual acuity using a pocket Snellen card, based on standard
         procedures CBVISN1 (NOT INCLUDED IN WAVE C) is always 20, and CBVISN2 represents
         the lower number of the Respondent's visual acuity relative to the standard of
         CBVISN1.  For instance, if the Respondent has 20/50 vision by Snellen card, they
         are estimated to be able to see at 20 feet what a person with normal vision can
         see at 50 feet.  Coding would be reflected as CBVISN1= 20 and CBVISN2= 50 (i.e.,
         20/50 vision).

         .................................................................................
           299                  10-400.  Feet
            15                     997.  Not assessed/Not asked
